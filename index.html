gmail-labels

gmail-labels is a personal tool. It is used only by its owner, to organise the labels of the owner's own Gmail account.

  • It runs on the owner's computer.
  • It reads messages and changes labels only in the owner's account.
  • It does not send, delete or share mail.
  • The data stays under the owner's sole control; nothing is shared with anyone.
  • It is not offered to anyone else.
