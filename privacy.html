Privacy policy

gmail-labels is a personal tool used only by its owner to organise the labels of the owner's own Gmail account. It is not offered to anyone else.

What data it accesses

It reads messages and their labels in the owner's Gmail account. In that account only, it adds and removes the owner's own labels, creates and removes filters that only add labels, and places its own status reports in the owner's mailbox. It does not access any other account.

What it does with the data

It runs on the owner's computer. It uses the data only to organise the labels of the owner's account. It does not send, delete or share mail.

Where the data goes

The data is used only by the owner. Apart from the requests to Google's API, it is kept only in places under the owner's sole control. Nothing is sold or shared with third parties.

Storage

Anything the tool stores stays under the owner's sole control. Access to the account can be revoked at any time from the Google Account security settings.
