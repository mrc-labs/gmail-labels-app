Privacy policy

gmail-labels is a personal tool used only by its owner to organise the labels of the owner's own Gmail account. It is not offered to anyone else.

What data it accesses

It reads messages and their labels in the owner's Gmail account, and changes labels only in that account. It does not access any other account.

What it does with the data

It runs on the owner's computer. It uses the data only to organise the labels of the owner's account. It does not send, delete or share mail.

Where the data goes

No data leaves the owner's computer except the requests to Google's API. Nothing is sent to any other service, and nothing is sold or shared with third parties.

Storage

Anything the tool stores stays on the owner's computer. Access to the account can be revoked at any time from the Google Account security settings.
